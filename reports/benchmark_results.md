# MediSentinel benchmark results

Generated by `python tools/benchmark.py`. The workload is loaded inside one transaction that is rolled back afterwards, so the database is unchanged.

**Data volume:** 20,103 patients · 100,305 prescriptions · 300,779 prescription items · 1,303 interaction rules · 30,088 alerts

Median of 5 runs of `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)`.

| Query | With indexes (ms) | Without indexes (ms) | Speed-up | Plan with indexes | Plan without |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 active alerts of one patient (Safety alerts page) | 7.775 | 17.861 | 2× | Seq Scan on alert_log; Bitmap Heap Scan on prescription; Bitmap Index Scan (idx_presc_patient); Index Scan on prescription_item (uq_item) | Seq Scan on alert_log; Seq Scan on prescription; Index Scan on prescription_item (uq_item) |
| Q2 active medicines of one patient (safety trigger and safety preview) | 0.064 | 9.145 | 143× | Bitmap Heap Scan on prescription; Bitmap Index Scan (idx_presc_patient); Index Only Scan on prescription_item (uq_item) | Seq Scan on prescription; Index Only Scan on prescription_item (uq_item) |
| Q3 interaction rule for one medicine pair (safety trigger) | 0.020 | 0.169 | 8× | Index Scan on interaction (idx_interaction) | Seq Scan on interaction |
| Q4 prescriptions containing one medicine (signal and usage reports) | 1.366 | 66.121 | 48× | Bitmap Heap Scan on prescription_item; Bitmap Index Scan (idx_item_med) | Seq Scan on prescription_item |

## Safety trigger cost per prescription item

300 single-row inserts, measured from the application.

| Trigger | Mean (ms) | 95th percentile (ms) |
|---|---|---|
| On (interaction, contraindication and allergy checks) | 0.94 | 1.04 |
| Off | 0.36 | 0.53 |

The safety check adds about 0.57 ms per medicine on this machine.
